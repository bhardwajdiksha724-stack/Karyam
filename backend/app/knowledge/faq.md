# Karyam FAQ

## Is my data secure?

Karyam uses password hashing (bcrypt) so raw passwords are never stored,
and JWT-based authentication to keep accounts secure. Access is also
restricted by role, so employees cannot see or change things only a
manager should control.

## Can I use Karyam without a credit card?

Yes. The Free tier requires no payment information and supports teams of
up to 3 people.

## Does the AI assistant make things up?

No. The assistant is designed to only answer using real data — either the
team's actual tasks and timesheets (for the in-app assistant) or Karyam's
actual documentation (for this landing-page assistant). If it doesn't have
enough information to answer a question, it will say so rather than
guessing.

## Can employees see the whole team's tasks?

Yes. Everyone on the team can see the full task board for visibility, but
only managers can create, reassign, or delete tasks, and only managers can
approve timesheets. Employees can only update the status of tasks assigned
to them and manage their own timesheet entries.

## What happens after the free trial of AI queries runs out?

On the Free tier, once the 10 monthly AI assistant queries are used, the
team can upgrade to Starter or Professional for higher or unlimited usage,
or continue using the rest of Karyam's features without the assistant
until the next month.

## Is there a mobile app?

Not currently — Karyam is a web application accessible from any browser.
