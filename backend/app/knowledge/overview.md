# Karyam Overview

Karyam is an AI-powered manager dashboard built for small to mid-size teams.
It replaces scattered spreadsheets, chat threads, and separate time-tracking
tools with one place to manage work.

## Who it's for

Small to mid-size teams — roughly 5 to 50 people — including startups,
agencies, and department-level teams inside larger companies. Karyam is
built for managers who need visibility into their team's workload without
manually chasing status updates.

## Core idea

Instead of a manager having to dig through boards, spreadsheets, and Slack
threads to understand what their team is working on, Karyam gives them a
live dashboard plus an AI assistant they can just ask directly — like
"what's overdue this week?" or "summarize the team's progress."

## How the AI assistant works

The AI assistant is grounded in real data. When a manager asks a question,
Karyam pulls the actual current tasks, timesheets, and team information from
its database and gives that to the AI model along with the question. This
means the assistant answers based on what's really happening on the team,
not by making things up.
