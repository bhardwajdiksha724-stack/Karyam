# Karyam Features

## Task Management

A Kanban-style task board with three columns: To Do, In Progress, and Done.
Managers can create tasks, assign them to team members, set a priority
(Low, Medium, or High), and give them a due date. Team members can update
the status of tasks assigned to them, for example moving a card from To Do
to In Progress as they start working on it.

## Timesheets

Team members log hours worked against specific tasks and dates. Managers
review these entries in a weekly view and approve or reject them. This
creates a simple, lightweight approval workflow without needing a separate
timesheet tool.

## Dashboard

A summary view showing total tasks, how many are completed, total hours
logged, and the size of the team. It also shows each team member's current
workload — how many open tasks they have — so a manager can see at a glance
who might be overloaded.

## AI Assistant

Two separate AI assistants exist in Karyam:

1. An assistant available to logged-in users, which answers questions about
   the actual team's tasks and timesheets, personalized to whoever is
   asking.
2. A separate assistant on the login page, available to visitors who
   haven't signed up yet, which answers questions about what Karyam is and
   how it works — this is the assistant currently answering your question.

## Roles and Permissions

Karyam supports two account types: Manager and Employee. Managers can
create, reassign, and delete tasks, and approve or reject timesheets.
Employees can update the status of tasks assigned to them and log their own
hours, but cannot approve timesheets or manage tasks that aren't theirs.
