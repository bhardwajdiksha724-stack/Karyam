# Karyam Pricing

Karyam offers four pricing plans, so there's an affordable option whatever
the cost or price you're looking for. Here's what each plan costs and
includes.

## Free — $0/month

Up to 3 team members. Includes the task board and basic timesheets. Limited
to 10 AI assistant queries per month, so a team can try the assistant before
upgrading.

## Starter — $6 per user per month

Unlimited team members. Full task and timesheet features. AI assistant
usage is capped at 100 queries per month for the team.

## Professional — $14 per user per month

Unlimited AI assistant usage. Adds timesheet approval workflows, exportable
reports, and priority customer support.

## Enterprise — Custom pricing

Includes single sign-on (SSO), dedicated onboarding, custom integrations,
a usage service-level agreement (SLA), and unlimited usage of every
feature. Enterprise pricing is arranged individually with each customer
rather than published.

## Why this pricing model

Karyam uses per-seat pricing because it's the standard, familiar model for
team collaboration software. The free tier is intentionally usable on its
own, so a single small team can start using Karyam for free and upgrade
naturally as they grow, rather than needing a large upfront company-wide
purchase decision.
